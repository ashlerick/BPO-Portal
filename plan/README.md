# Plan folder

Documents about what the BPO Portal and BPO Time are, how they are protected, and what is planned. Written for HR and
for whoever builds next. The long design documents (`bpo_plan.md`, `new_plan.md`, `desktop_app_plan.md`) and the
hand-over steps (`HANDOFF.md`, `STAGING.md`) stay at the top of the repository.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [hr-guide.md](./hr-guide.md) | A plain guide for HR: what the portal and the app do, the rules we set, where to do each HR task, and what is waiting for HR's decision |
| [security-and-privacy.md](./security-and-privacy.md) | What we use (services), how people sign in, who can see what, how the app is protected, what we record, and an honest list of gaps |
| [holidays.md](./holidays.md) | Research on national and Panay holidays, the free data sources, and the plan for holidays in the portal |

Keep these up to date when a rule or a service changes. The date at the top of each file says when it was last checked.
